## 1. Project objective and positioning

Build a clear, credible, responsive digital presence for **ADIDEV**,
positioned as an engineering and infrastructure solutions company.

The website must explain:

-   What Adidev does.
-   Where it works.
-   How it delivers projects.
-   What capabilities and services it offers.
-   How prospective clients, vendors, and partners can engage with the
    company.

### Core business verticals

1.  Telecom Infrastructure
2.  Solar & Renewable Energy
3.  Civil Construction & Infrastructure
4.  Commercial Interior & Fit-Out

### Website goals

-   **Company identity:** Position Adidev as an engineering and
    infrastructure solutions company.
-   **Four business verticals:** Present telecom, solar, civil
    infrastructure, and commercial interior fit-out as clear service
    areas.
-   **Proof of capability:** Showcase the project portfolio, technical
    strengths, people, quality practices, and compliance.
-   **Business conversion:** Make contact details, enquiry forms, and
    quote requests easy to find and use.

**Positioning principle:** Communicate verified capability clearly.
Avoid unsupported claims and inflated numbers.

## 9. Execution approach

Create a five-stage process graphic so clients can understand how a
project moves from requirement to handover.

### Stage 1 --- Understand

Client brief, objectives, site conditions, scope, and constraints.

### Stage 2 --- Plan

Drawings, BOQ, estimates, resources, schedule, and technical
coordination.

### Stage 3 --- Mobilise

Approvals, procurement, vendors, manpower, equipment, and site
readiness.

### Stage 4 --- Execute

Site coordination, progress monitoring, quality checks, and safe work
practices.

### Stage 5 --- Inspect & Handover

Inspections, testing where applicable, snag closure, documents, and
handover.

    status.

    regional offices, social links, enquiry recipient.

content requirements; they are not claims that the company already has a
CMS or these records.
